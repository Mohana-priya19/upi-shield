About UPI Shield

A Production-Ready MLOps Pipeline for Real-Time UPI Fraud Detection

Problem Statement

UPI transactions in India process over 10 billion payments monthly. Fraud detection in this ecosystem requires real-time decisions, explainable predictions, and production-grade monitoring. Existing research stops at model accuracy — UPI Shield goes further by implementing the complete MLOps lifecycle from raw data to live monitoring.

Our Approach

1. Domain-Specific Feature Engineering
7 features engineered from balance draining behavior — balance_diff_orig and orig_balance_zero emerged as the two strongest fraud signals from EDA.
2. Dual-Model Architecture
XGBoost learns from labeled fraud patterns. Isolation Forest detects structural anomalies without labels — covering novel fraud patterns XGBoost cannot anticipate.
3. Full MLOps Pipeline
MLflow experiment tracking, FastAPI serving, SHAP explainability, Evidently AI drift monitoring, unit testing — every stage of production ML covered.
