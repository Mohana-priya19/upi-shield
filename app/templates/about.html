About UPI Shield

Real-time fraud detection built for the scale of UPI.

The Problem

Rule-based fraud systems miss behavioural patterns fraudsters actively evolve around. Even where machine learning is used, models silently go stale as transaction patterns shift — and most research stops at accuracy, never reaching a deployable pipeline.

The Approach

UPI Shield combines a supervised XGBoost classifier with an unsupervised Isolation Forest to catch both known fraud patterns and novel anomalies. Every prediction is explained with SHAP, and Evidently AI continuously benchmarks live data against NPCI's official statistics to catch drift before it silently degrades the model.
